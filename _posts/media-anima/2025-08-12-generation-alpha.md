### Media anima

<u>Media</u>: Learned borrowing from Latin media, the feminine nominative of medius (“middle”, adjective), Proto-Indo-European *médʰyos (“between”).
<br>
<u>Anima</u>: Borrowed from Latin anima (“a current of air, wind, air, breath, the vital principle, life, soul”), sometimes equivalent to animus (“mind”), both from Proto-Indo-European *h₂enh₁- (“to breathe, blow”).
